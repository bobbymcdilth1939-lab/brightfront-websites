Privacy note

This is a short, plain-English note about how this website handles information.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, tracking or forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages while in preview, and the host may keep ordinary server logs.

If you email us

If you email brightfrontwebsites@gmail.com, we use your email address and what you tell us only to reply to you and to discuss a possible website. We don't sell or share it. Email is handled by Google (Gmail).

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, or to delete it, by emailing us. If you ask us to stop emailing you, we will.
