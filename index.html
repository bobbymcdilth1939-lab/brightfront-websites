Questions

Do I need my own domain?

No, but we recommend one, such as yourbusiness.co.uk. You buy it from a registrar and pay for it yourself; we help connect it to your site.

Where is my site hosted?

On Cloudflare Pages, a fast and secure hosting platform.

Are there monthly fees?

No. The packages are one-off prices. The only other cost is your domain name, if you want one.

Do you only work in Bromley?

No. We're based in Bromley, but we work with businesses anywhere, all by email.

What is the free mock-up?

A free, no-obligation mock-up of your homepage so you can see what we'd do before deciding anything.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, any text and photos you have, and your logo if you have one. We can help with the wording.
